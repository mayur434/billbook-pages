About Bill Book

Bill Book is a private billing app used internally by Girish B. Parmar (Palghar East, Maharashtra) to create and send invoices to clients of his sewing-machine-parts business.

It is not distributed publicly, has no public sign-up, and is not available on any app store. It is used on a single device by its owner.

What it does

  • Creates bills for clients and shares them on WhatsApp
  • Confirms each signed bill with the owner's own fingerprint/face
  • Keeps a private backup of business records on the owner's personal Google Drive
