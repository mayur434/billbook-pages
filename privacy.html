Who this applies to

Bill Book is a private app used by a single business owner, Girish B. Parmar, for his own invoicing. It is not distributed to the public and has no other users or accounts.

What data the app stores

The app stores business records — clients, products, invoices, and the owner's signature image and business details — on the owner's own phone first. This data never leaves the device except to the owner's own personal Google Drive, described below.

Google Drive backup

The app backs up the owner's business data to the owner's personal Google Drive account, using Google's restricted drive.file scope. Under this scope, the app can only see and modify files that it itself created — it cannot read, list, or modify any other file in the Drive account, and no other app or person is given access through this integration.

Sharing with others

No data is sold, shared, or made available to any third party. The only sharing the app performs is initiated directly by the owner — for example, sending a bill to a client over WhatsApp, or creating a view-only link to a single bill PDF so a client can view it.

Data retention and deletion

Business records stay on the device and in the owner's Drive for as long as the owner keeps the app installed and connected. Deleting the app, or disconnecting Google Drive in Settings, stops any further backup. The owner can also delete individual bills from within the app at any time.
